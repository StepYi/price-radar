**摘要**

Abstract Central banks increasingly disclose their private information regarding the economy’s current and future state (CBET). Consistent with CBET shifting investors’ collection and pricing of aggregate-level (where managers do not have an information advantage) toward firm-level (where managers do have an information advantage) information, we find that CBET reduces managers’ learning from stock price. **摘要**

ABSTRACT I show that the recent returns to environmental, social, and governance (ESG) investing are strongly driven by price impact from flows toward ESG portfolios. Using data on trades, I estimate the market's ability to accommodate ESG flows, which is given by the elasticity of substitution between ESG and other stocks. I show that every dollar flowing toward a representative ESG portfolio increases the market value of ESG stocks by $0.8. In the absence of flows, ESG stocks would not have outperformed the market from 2012 to 2023.

## 9. Tourism Demand Prediction With Tourist‐Generated Images and Text: An Ensemble Sparse ELM Approach

**Journal of Forecasting** · 上线 2026-10-13（1 天前） · T3 · Chuan Zhang、Yun‐Xiao Zhao、Yu‐Xin Tian · 综合分 **37.1** · 中等相关

标签：`yield` `yields`

**摘要**

ABSTRACT This study examines the predictive value of tourist‐generated images and text for daily tourism demand forecasting. Building on established L 0 ‐regularized extreme learning machine (ELM) formulations and ADMM‐based sparse optimization, we construct an ensemble forecasting framework, termed ES‐ L 0 ‐ELM, that combines image‐derived and textual predictors with numerical variables. The framework integrates sparse output‐weight estimation with model selection and weighted aggregation. Experiments on Jiuzhaigou and Mount Siguniang show that ES‐ L 0 ‐ELM achieves lower forecasting errors than the evaluated benchmarks. On the Jiuzhaigou dataset, it reduces MAPE by 18.66% and 14.33% relative to recent tourism forecasting models BERT‐ELM and TFIDF‐Tran‐ELM, respectively. Image features provide greater predictive gains than text features, whereas their combination yields the best performance. This study provides a sparse ensemble forecasting framework and empirical evidence of the complementary value of tourist‐generated images and text.

## 10. The Origin of Risk

**The Quarterly Journal of Economics** · 上线 2026-09-30（14 天前） · T1 · Alexandr Kopytov、Mathieu Taschereau-Dumouchel、Zebang Xu · 综合分 **36.7** · 中等相关

标签：`markup` `markups`

**摘要**

Abstract We propose a tractable model in which risk, at both the micro and macro levels, is endogenous and driven by incentives. In the model, each firm chooses the mean and the variance of its productivity process, as well as how it covaries with the productivity of other firms. Aggregate risk arises when firms select productivity processes that are correlated with one another. The theory predicts that larger firms and those with lower markups are less volatile and less correlated with aggregate productivity. We find support for these predictions in the data. Through their impact on risk-taking decisions, distortions such as taxes and markups can make GDP more volatile in equilibrium. In a calibrated version of the model, removing distortions significantly reduces GDP volatility.
