标签：`price` `energy price` `yield` `yields` `brent` `wti` `oil` `coal`

**摘要**

ABSTRACT In this study, we evaluate Autometrics, an automated model selection method with controls for outliers and location shifts, against artificial neural networks (ANN) and random forests for six monthly energy price series (WTI, Brent, Dubai, US and Russian gas, Australian coal) from IMF data. Autometrics generally delivers lower RMSE/MAPE and higher directional accuracy than the ANN and random forest specifications considered here, particularly in the more volatile oil and coal markets. Autometrics also has the benefit of being interpretable in a familiar regression context as opposed to more “blackbox” machine learning. The CARDt method often delivers predictive accuracy which is not statistically different from Autometrics, but there is some evidence that simple model averaging of the two with intercept‐corrected Autometrics yields further gains relative to either alone.

## 3. Contracts in crisis: The war in Ukraine and long-term contracts in energy markets

**Resource and Energy Economics** · 上线 2026-11-09（3 天前） · T2 · Mats Kröger、Karsten Neuhoff、Sebastian Schwenen · 综合分 **34.9** · 中等相关

标签：`price` `price shock` `price shocks` `hedging`

**摘要**

We examine the impact of the war in Ukraine on long-term contracts in energy markets. We find that traded contract volumes fall by 65 percent in the first months of the war. A collapse in bilateral trading contributes most to this decline. To protect themselves from price shocks, firms increasingly turned to long-term contracts already before the war. In sum, our results show that the market continued to serve firms’ hedging needs during the crisis, but bilateral trading networks collapsed, and liquidity was largely provided by centralized markets.

## 4. Coherent forecast combination for linearly constrained multiple time series

**International Journal of Forecasting** · 上线 2026-11-06（6 天前） · T2 · Daniele Girolimetto、Tommaso Di Fonzo · 综合分 **31.5** · 强相关

标签：`electricity`

**摘要**

Forecasting linearly constrained multiple time series, as those arising in National Accounts, energy systems and supply chain planning, requires that the forecasts of all variables jointly satisfy the underlying accounting or resource-balance identities. When several experts provide forecasts for some or all of the variables, classical forecast combination operates on each variable and ignores the linear constraints, while forecast reconciliation enforces such constraints by using a single forecast per variable. To bridge this gap, we develop an original coherent forecast combination framework, in which the joint problem of combination across experts and coherence across variables is formulated as a linearly constrained optimization problem. Closed-form expressions are derived for the coherent combined forecast and its error covariance matrix, and the statistical properties of the resulting estimators are discussed. A Monte Carlo experiment and a forecasting application to an Australian daily electricity generation dataset confirm that the proposed approach significantly improves forecast accuracy with respect to standard forecast combination and reconciliation alternatives.
