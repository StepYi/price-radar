## 1. Presidential Address: Human Finance: Incorporating Insights from the Life Sciences

**The Journal of Finance** · 上线 2026-11-10（1 天前） · 在线 2026-11-09 · T1 · ULRIKE MALMENDIER · 综合分 **41.0** · 弱相关

标签：`inflation`

**摘要**

ABSTRACT Many of the key determinants of human behavior identified in the life sciences, such as stress response, impulse control, and mood regulation, remain absent from standard economic and finance models. This paper illustrates how such insights can enhance economic models in the context of inflation expectations. Combining a retrospective survey that elicits personal inflation histories with an experimental intervention that induces the reexperiencing of historic inflation, I show that physiological, emotional, and cognitive stress markers predict heightened inflation expectations and sharper memory recall, while also shaping personal economic narratives. These findings suggest novel policy tools to counter the persistent scars of economic distress and crisis experiences, for example, by designing experiences to provide predictability and control.

## 2. When Insurers Exit: Climate Losses, Fragile Insurers, and Mortgage Markets

**The Quarterly Journal of Economics** · 上线 2026-11-07（4 天前） · 在线 2026-11-10 · T1 · Parinitha Sastry、Ishita Sen、Ana-Maria Tenekedjieva · 综合分 **37.1** · 弱相关

标签：`pricing`

**摘要**

Abstract This paper uses a range of natural experiments to show how mispricing of climate risk in mortgages and property insurance creates large taxpayer exposures and leads to excess credit flows to risky areas. Our central finding is that the government-sponsored enterprises’ (GSEs) policies for evaluating property insurers are a crucial source of this mispricing. We assemble a comprehensive new dataset on both mortgages and insurance to analyze these dynamics in Florida from 2009-2018. We begin by documenting a breakdown in the quality of insurance provision, with new under-capitalized and under-diversified insurers dominating insurance markets. These fragile insurers have high rates of insolvency, which we show causally increases mortgage defaults after natural disasters. We find that the GSEs’ reliance on third-party ratings of insurers leads them to accept insurance from companies at high risk of insolvency without pricing for it. This creates large taxpayer exposures, with an estimated 25% of the GSEs’ expected losses in Florida coming from insurance fragility. Most starkly, we show that private lenders strategically respond to the GSE mispricing. Mortgage denial rates are sensitive to insurance quality in the jumbo segment where loans are retained, but not in the conforming segment where loans are offloaded to the GSEs. This suggests that, all else equal, fewer conforming mortgages would have been originated if lenders screened insurance fragility as they do in jumbos.

## 3. Deadly Disease, Fear, and Ostracism in the Credit Market

**Management Science** · 上线 2026-11-10（1 天前） · T1 · Sumit Agarwal、Pengsheng Lin、Yupeng Lin、Ruichang Lu 等 5 人 · 综合分 **35.4** · 强相关

**摘要**

This paper exploits the 2014 U.S. Ebola outbreak as a natural experiment to examine how fear distorts mortgage credit allocation. We find a 3.8-percentage-point increase in mortgage denial rates for Black applicants during the outbreak, despite negligible infection risk. Approved Black borrowers have stronger credit profiles and lower default rates, yet are less likely to receive loan modifications when delinquent. These effects are absent in algorithm-driven FinTech lending and reverse quickly postoutbreak—patterns inconsistent with statistical discrimination. Robustness tests rule out alternative explanations related to political cycles, labor market impacts, applicant pool composition changes, and bank financial conditions. Consistent with diagnostic expectation theory, higher exposure to Ebola fear does not result in a significant learning effect during the COVID-19 pandemic. ## 4. Uncertainty Attitudes, Subjective Beliefs, and Decisions Under Uncertainty

**Management Science** · 上线 2026-11-10（1 天前） · T1 · Adeline Delavande、Jayant Ganguli、Friederike Mengel · 综合分 **35.4** · 强相关

**摘要**

We conducted survey experiments to gather new data on uncertainty attitudes and subjective beliefs. Our survey targets a general population, focusing on health and financial decisions. We collect data on (i) individual attitudes toward risk and ambiguity, including individual-specific probability weighting and loss aversion, and (ii) individual probabilistic beliefs about events related to the decisions we consider. The joint availability of individual preference parameters and subjective beliefs allows us to study heterogeneity and cross-correlations across these parameters in depth and to estimate structural models of decision making under uncertainty that go beyond subjective expected utility. We find that both preferences and beliefs are highly heterogeneous. Compared with subjective expected utility, prospect theory combined with source-dependent probability weighting fits the data strictly better in some contexts and equally well in others. Prospect theory performs particularly well when the belief distribution is concentrated toward extreme values. ## 5. Channeled Attention and Stable Errors

**The Quarterly Journal of Economics** · 上线 2026-11-09（2 天前） · 在线 2026-11-10 · T1 · Tristan Gagnon-Bartsch、Matthew Rabin、Joshua Schwartzstein · 综合分 **33.9** · 强相关

**摘要**

Abstract People persist in mistaken beliefs even in settings with high stakes, abundant data, and repeated opportunities to learn—settings where standard arguments imply they should correct their errors. We develop an attention-based framework for understanding why errors persist. When a person channels his attention through the lens of his potentially misspecified model, exposure to data is not the same as tracking the statistics necessary to tell him his model is wrong. A model is attentionally stable when the data the person deems relevant will, in the long run, fail to make the model look implausible. Models that erroneously rule things out—e.g., beliefs that a variable doesn’t matter or that a distinction doesn’t exist—tend to be attentionally stable because they divert attention away from data that would refute the models. Conversely, models that erroneously rule things in are less likely to be stable. Whether an error is discovered depends on the structure of the decision problem but is not tightly linked to its level of harm; arbitrarily costly errors can persist while trivially inexpensive ones may be detected. Nevertheless, no error is robust both to changes in the decision environment and to shocks that expand attention: every channeled-attention error is vulnerable to some perturbation that would reveal it. We use the framework to analyze the stability of psychological biases and empirical misconceptions, as well as to explain why fresh eyes catch mistakes, why people use overly coarse rather than overly fine categorizations, and why people recognize errors in others that they miss in themselves.

## 6. Recommender Systems Under Privacy Protection

**Management Science** · 上线 2026-11-10（1 天前） · T1 · Can Küçükgül、Özalp Özer、Shouqiang Wang · 综合分 **32.9** · 强相关

**摘要**

We examine how a profit-driven platform designs its recommender policies under varying privacy protection regimes. We demonstrate a functional equivalence between opt-out protection and unprotected privacy. Notably, granting consumers the autonomy to misrepresent personal data can compel the platform to distort recommendations, inadvertently harming both parties. When recommendations are purely informative, such privacy controls can deter personalization entirely. However, when recommendations also serve an allocative role—controlling product access—algorithmic discrimination may emerge, restricting the disadvantaged minority from valuable opportunities. These findings remain robust even with endogenous profit margins. This paper was accepted by Omar Besbes, revenue management and market analytics.
