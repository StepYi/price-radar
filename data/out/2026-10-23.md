## 2. Excess Movement in Option‐Implied Beliefs

**The Journal of Finance** · 上线 2026-10-22（1 天前） · T1 · NED AUGENBLICK、EBEN LAZARUS · 综合分 **54.2** · 中等相关

标签：`price` `option` `options`

**摘要**

ABSTRACT We derive bounds on the rational variation in option‐implied beliefs about market returns. While risk preferences distort option‐implied (or risk‐neutral ) beliefs away from subjective beliefs, one can nonetheless bound risk‐neutral belief movement under a general assumption on the stochastic discount factor. The resulting test requires no knowledge of the objective distribution and allows significantly more flexibility in discount rates than standard volatility tests. Implementing our test empirically using index options, we find that there is so much movement in risk‐neutral beliefs that the bounds are routinely violated. Our results imply significant excess index‐price volatility.

## 3. From Winning to Giving: The Effect of Gold Medals in Crowdsourcing Contests

**Management Science** · 上线 2026-10-22（1 天前） · T1 · Shan Liu、Fan Zhang、Jing Wang、Baojun Gao · 综合分 **51.1** · 中等相关

标签：`gold` `silver`

**摘要**

We examine how users respond to winning symbolic awards in online crowdsourcing contests. Our setting is a machine learning competition platform that grants symbolic medals to recognize participants’ contest achievements. Employing a regression discontinuity design, we find that gold medal winners participate less in subsequent contests but engage more in prosocial activities, evidenced by a greater volume and length of posts, as well as more substantial and frequent code contributions. In contrast, bronze and silver medals do not seem to exert a significant influence on recipients’ subsequent behaviors. Drawing on self-determination theory and the needs-as-motives hypothesis, we provide evidence that need-driven goal shifting plays an important role in driving the dual effects of gold medals—diminished contest participation but enhanced prosocial contributions. Further analyses reveal that winners with previous competition medals are more likely to favor contributing content over competing again, whereas winners with more prior contribution medals are less inclined to further increase contributions. Moreover, team winners exhibit a lower tendency than solo winners to shift their efforts from competition to contribution. We add to the symbolic awards literature by uncovering a novel goal-shifting effect induced by contest medals, where award receipt redirects user effort from the focal rewarded domain to other forms of platform engagement. **摘要**

Economists estimate that zoning regulations raise house prices, lower productivity, and reduce aggregate welfare, yet regulations remain widespread. We develop and solve a model in which fragmented zoning authority leads to overly strict regulations: Homeowners impose stricter regulations to mitigate local congestion but fail to internalize the broader impact on metro-wide affordability. Empirically, the Herfindahl-Hirschman index of local governments explains 12% of the variation in zoning restrictiveness. Using an instrument based on 19th-century municipal fragmentation, we show that greater fragmentation leads to stricter zoning and higher housing costs. ## 5. Information Transparency, Choice Confidence, and Customer Retention: Field and Laboratory Evidence from Markets for Credence Goods

**Management Science** · 上线 2026-10-22（1 天前） · T1 · Zhi (Aaron) Cheng、Ting Li、Paul A. Pavlou · 综合分 **38.3** · 弱相关

标签：`price`

**摘要**

How does information transparency affect customer retention in markets where quality is difficult to verify? Conventional wisdom suggests that transparency intensifies price competition and increases customer churn. We challenge this view by showing that acquisition-stage transparency can strengthen retention in markets for credence goods characterized by delayed feedback and persistent uncertainty, where customers revisit their decisions without clear quality signals. We develop a transparency-confidence-retention framework, in which transparency at acquisition builds choice confidence, a psychological switching cost that anchors customers to their initial decisions. Using 83,835 auto insurance contracts from a European insurer and multiple identification strategies, we find that customers acquired through transparent channels are 4.6%–11.8% less likely to churn than those acquired through less transparent channels. A randomized, controlled experiment with 1,515 participants shows that transparency reduces customer churn primarily by strengthening choice confidence rather than increasing conventional switching costs, such as brand trust or product fit. Decomposing transparency into attribute, comparative, and evaluative dimensions reveals that evaluative transparency exerts the strongest influence. Analyses of boundary conditions indicate that the retention effect of transparency is amplified when public and private signals remain congruent with earlier informational cues. These findings reconceptualize transparency as a relational mechanism that fosters decisional assurance and stabilizes retention under quality uncertainty. ## 6. From Bytes to Bites: Examining the Online and Offline Spillover Impacts of Online Food Delivery Platform Subscriptions

**摘要**

With the rapid expansion of online food delivery (OFD) platforms, subscription models have emerged as a strategic tool to retain users and shape consumption behavior. Despite their growing prevalence, the broader impacts of these subscriptions—both within the OFD sector and across downstream consumer activities—remain underexplored. Leveraging a difference-in-differences approach and a large-scale individual-level transaction data set, we identify a significant shift in consumption from a competing platform to the focal platform following the introduction of a subscription service. This shift did not result in cannibalization; instead, overall food consumption within the OFD market increased. Geospatial analysis shows that subscribers were more likely to engage in offline spillovers, including increased local dining and activity in nearby noncompeting economic sectors. These patterns suggest that subscriptions in physical services, like OFD, not only reshape digital engagement, but also, they influence consumer mobility and stimulate localized economic ecosystems. Mechanistic testing supports the interpretation that these effects are driven by increased variety-seeking behavior for offline restaurants that predominantly serve non-OFD cuisines within a proximal location, suggesting that such behavior is constrained by transportation costs. Overall, this study offers novel insights into how subscription models in physical service platforms can expand market demand, foster crosschannel engagement, and generate positive spillovers in adjacent sectors. The findings offer actionable guidance for platform designers and policymakers to enhance consumer welfare and support local economic vitality. They also provide new evidence for restaurants that additional platform usage costs may be offset by latent economic benefits from heightened consumer engagement. This paper was accepted by D. J. ## 8. Household Effects of Electrification Through Mini-Grids: Evidence from Tanzania

标签：`renewable` `agriculture`

**摘要**

Mini-grids are emerging as a key solution to electrify access-deficit communities, yet their effectiveness in improving energy access and household welfare remains underexplored. This paper provides novel evidence from Tanzania, where a policy reform doubled the number of mini-grids since 2008. Exploiting spatial and temporal variation created by the distance to mini-grids for households in proximity and the timing of their deployment, and using data from two different nationally representative surveys, we find that mini-grids increase local electrification rates by 10 to 23 percentage points—a result corroborated by a surge in nighttime light intensity near newly deployed projects. We also show that mini-grids reduce reliance on polluting fuel-based lighting and drive the uptake of electric-powered devices. Back-of-the-envelope calculations suggest the surplus generated by renewable-based mini-grids nearly offsets their costs. ## 9. How New Products Enter Markets: Social Networks, Consumer Imports, and Retailer Learning

**The Quarterly Journal of Economics** · 上线 2026-10-20（3 天前） · 在线 2026-10-22 · T1 · David Argente、Esteban Méndez、Diana Van Patten · 综合分 **35.3** · 强相关

**摘要**

Abstract This paper studies how new varieties enter markets and become locally available. We provide causal evidence of demand externalities that operate in two steps. First, information about new varieties diffuses directly through real-world social ties among consumers. Second, early purchases generate an indirect spillover to firms: local retailers learn from “pioneer” consumers which new varieties are most likely to succeed and adjust their product offerings accordingly. We study this process in the context of direct-to-consumer imports. Using customs records on individuals’ purchases matched to population-wide social networks, international migrant links, and retailer catchment areas, we document economically meaningful demand externalities. Product-specific expenditure shocks abroad transmit through migrant networks and shift which varieties consumers purchase. Leveraging these shocks as a plausibly exogenous source of local demand variation, we show strong peer effects: prior purchases by close neighbors, coworkers, or friends increase an individual’s likelihood of purchasing the same variety, especially for premium and visible goods. We leverage this result to identify an indirect spillover from consumers to firms: retailers are more likely to add a variety to their assortment when it becomes popular among consumers in their catchment area. Combining the instrument with linked consumer–retailer data and a self-conducted retailer survey, we show that this response reflects learning about latent demand for varieties not yet stocked locally. Together, social diffusion and retailer learning generate demand multipliers that reshape local product availability and expand access to global variety.

## 10. Optimal Flood Insurance in a Second-Best World: Fiscal Spillovers, Reclassification Risk, and Moral Hazard

**The Quarterly Journal of Economics** · 上线 2026-10-20（3 天前） · 在线 2026-10-22 · T1 · Jonathan Gruber、Adam Solomon · 综合分 **35.3** · 强相关

**摘要**

Abstract Intensifying climate change makes protection against natural disaster risk – either through ex ante insurance or ex post aid – a first-order public policy issue. Flood risk protection in the U.S. traditionally featured ex post aid through FEMA and heavily subsidized insurance through the National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP). In an effort to correct subsidy-induced overbuilding and under-mitigation in flood-prone areas, the NFIP recently moved to actuarially fair insurance premiums. But this change has two unintended consequences: a fiscal spillover onto FEMA disaster aid as insurance coverage declines in flood-prone areas, and household exposure to uninsurable reclassification risk from uncertain climate projections. We develop a model of optimal flood insurance that incorporates the static and dynamic tradeoffs of subsidizing premiums. We then use a variety of identification strategies and data to estimate the five key parameters needed to implement this model. We find that the fiscal spillover and reclassification risk protection benefits significantly outweigh the moral hazard costs, and that the optimal subsidy to flood insurance is 51%, comparable to the level of subsidization before the recent reform.
